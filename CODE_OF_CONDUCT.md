# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:
- **Be kind and constructive.** Critique designs and code, not people.
- **Help newcomers.** Many people here are new to CAD, 3D printing or GitHub.
- **No harassment.** No insults or personal attacks, and no publishing other people's private information.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers by opening a private [security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) or by contacting a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content or ban participants who break these rules.
