    The AESS standard

    Every AESS part follows the same rules, so anything stacks and snaps onto anything else.

    Footprintn × 42 mm − 0.5, 1–6 units each way
    Heightnh × 7 mm + 4.3; each bin adds exactly nh × 7 to a stack
    LidThe same part at nh = 1: snaps on and takes another bin on top
    Foot4 mm deep with a 45° flare, so no supports
    RimAlways 1.2 mm in the snap zone, whatever the wall
    Snap0.6 mm bead on the foot, 0.4 mm groove inside the rim
    WallsLite 0.8 · Standard 1.2 · Heavy 1.4 mm

    Print settings

    0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers (0.28 for speed). 2 wall loops for Lite, 3 for Standard and Heavy. 0% infill, no supports. Print it as exported: foot down.
